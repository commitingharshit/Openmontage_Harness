# V21 script draft: open loop + Before-After-Bridge

Team feedback: the start can be better; amplify the problem; 5-second hook (visual anchor, then a curiosity loop); the reviewer preferred **Curiosity Loop** and **Before-After-Bridge**; end on a good slogan.

Film type: **top-of-funnel awareness** ad for consultants, finance teams and lawyers. Every number is from the real demo (one model change, 10 changes, 3 files), so nothing is invented.

## Structure

| Part | Job | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open loop | Visual anchor + a question we don't answer yet | 0–5 s |
| **Before** | The frustrating reality, made bigger | 5–16 s |
| **After** | The ideal future, shown before we say how | 16–22 s |
| **Bridge** | Agentic Office: how it gets you there (real UI) | 22–48 s |
| Loop closed | Answer the opening question | 48–53 s |
| Slogan + action | One line people remember, then one CTA | 53–60 s |

## Hook options (0–5 s): pick one

| | Visual anchor (first frame) | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A · Recommended** | One spreadsheet cell flips **€71–73m → €74–76m**; a red ripple spreads to the memo, the deck and the investor update | "One cell changed at 9 p.m. By morning, it had broken ten places in three files." |
| B | Memo, model and deck side by side; the same figure disagrees in each | "Three files. One number. Can you spot what's wrong?" |
| C | Typing into a chatbot; a long to-do list scrolls back | "We asked an AI to update our board pack. It gave us homework." |

Avoid the "I tried every productivity hack for 30 days" style unless a real person actually did it: finance and legal viewers read it as a fake testimonial.

## Full script (Hook A), about 60 s

| Time | Part | Voiceover | On screen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Open loop | One cell changed at 9 p.m. By morning, it had broken ten places in three files. | Cell flips; red ripple to three files; counter "10 wrong" |
| 5–16 | **Before** | It's in the memo. The model. Slide four of the deck. Miss one, and your lender sees two different numbers. So you hunt, file by file, line by line. Or you ask a chatbot, and it hands you a to-do list. | Team opening: stacked windows, mismatched numbers flash red, chat reply "update each file yourself" |
| 16–22 | **After** | Now imagine changing it once, and watching every file catch up, waiting only for your OK. | Sync wall: memo, model and deck light up along one beam, each with a pending "Review" tag |
| 22–28 | **Bridge** | That's Agentic Office. Your Word, Excel and PowerPoint files in one workspace, with an assistant that does the fixing. | White dip, logo, real app |
| 28–34 | Bridge | Tell it what changed. It finds every place that number lives, and drafts each fix. | Prompt typed; assistant steps; files listed |
| 34–41 | Bridge | Nothing changes without you. Every edit is marked, in the memo, the model and the deck. Accept it, or reject it. | Full-app review: Word → Excel bar → PowerPoint → Accept all |
| 41–48 | Bridge | Your team's assistants share what they know and hand off the work. Only a person says yes. | Assistants diagram → Accept in the Room |
| 48–53 | Loop closed | Ten places. Three files. One review. | Counter "10 of 10 accepted · 3 of 3 files", sync wall settles |
| 53–60 | Slogan + action | Agentic Office. **Change it once. Every file follows.** | Logo lockup, slogan, then the CTA card: "Request early access → [link]" (wording and link to be confirmed) |

## Slogan options (end line)

| | Slogan | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Recommended** | **Change it once. Every file follows.** | Answers the hook directly; plain words; easy to remember |
| 2 | One change. Every file. Your call. | Adds the control promise in three beats |
| 3 | Your files, in sync. You, in control. | Closest to the current ending |
| 4 | Stop fixing files. Start finishing work. | Benefit-led; slightly more "ad" in tone |

Slogan 1 can sit above "You stay in control." if the team wants the control message kept on the final card.

## Optional beat (adds about 6 s)

"Need something new? Ask for a five-slide lender deck. It builds it from your own files." (real prompt → deck scroll). Cut to keep the film near 60 s; keep it if the team wants "creates" as well as "fixes".

## Claims check

- "Ten places / three files": the real task-3 recording shows 10 changes across FY26 Model, Board Memo and Board Deck.
- "Every file follows" is always paired with review: the assistant drafts, a person accepts. No autonomous approval.
- "Stored on your device" (if used) always appears with "Cloud AI may process document content."
- "Assistant" is used instead of "agent" for lay viewers; the app itself labels them "Your Assistant" / "Priya's Assistant".
