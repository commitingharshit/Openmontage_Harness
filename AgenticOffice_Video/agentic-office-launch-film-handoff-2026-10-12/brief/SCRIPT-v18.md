# V18 script: light theme, plain-language rewrite

Audience: consultants, finance teams and lawyers. One plain story anyone can follow: **one number changes, and every file that uses it has to change too.**
Structure follows Divya's trial cut (number hook, chatbot problem, meet the product, it does the work, you approve, in sync, close). The look stays our own: light paper, ink and teal.

Voice: local Kokoro `af_heart` (draft). Replace with a premium voice before release.

| # | Voiceover | On screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Hook | It's Thursday night. One number in your model changes. | Big "€71–73m", struck through and replaced by "€74–76m" |
| | Now your memo is wrong. Your deck is wrong. Your investor update is wrong. | Three documents land, each tagged "Out of date" in red |
| 2 · Problem | So you paste it all into a chatbot. It tells you what to fix, and leaves the fixing to you. | Generic chat window (illustration): a to-do list reply. Chip "0 of 3 files updated" |
| 3 · Meet | Meet Agentic Office. Your documents, spreadsheets and slides, in one workspace, with an agent that does the work. | Logo, then the workspace window. Small print: "Files stored on your device. Cloud AI may process document content." |
| 4 · It works | Tell it what changed. It reads your files, finds every place that number appears, and drafts each fix. | Assistant panel: "The model moved. Update the board pack." / "What I did". Model: "Used in 3 documents" |
| 5 · You approve | Nothing changes without you. Every edit is marked. Accept it, or reject it. | Memo with a tracked edit, Accept / Reject highlighted |
| 6 · Team | Your team works in the same files. Their agents post a plan, and every proposal waits for a human yes. | Live session: who's here, Room thread, the agent's plan and proposal |
| 7 · In sync | Every file, in sync. The memo, the model, the deck, and no one copied a number by hand. | Memo, model and deck linked by one light beam. Chip "3 of 3 files updated" |
| 8 · Close | Agentic Office. Your files. Your team. Your agents. One workspace. You stay in control. | Logo lockup |

## Claims kept honest

- The agent **drafts** fixes; a person accepts or rejects them. No autonomous sending or approval.
- "Stored on your device" always appears with "Cloud AI may process document content."
- All UI here is from the light-mode Figma design set and is labelled **Design prototype** until a real light-mode recording exists.
- The chatbot window is a generic illustration, not a named competitor.
- Dropped from V17: the new lender deck built from a prompt (one story instead of two). It can come back as a scene if the team wants it.
