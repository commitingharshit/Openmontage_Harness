# Agentic Office — final content script

Content baseline: V11, 8 October 2026. Handoff: 10 October 2026. 64.8 seconds · 16:9 · 1920×1080 · 30 fps.

**Main idea:** Word, Excel, and PowerPoint work together in one agentic, collaborative workspace. Follow one consultant's work from memo and forecast to a presentation, review, teamwork, and agent handoff. No photographic person is needed to tell that story.

The voiceover below matches the existing V11 narration request exactly. The OpenArt visual upgrade is planned separately; it has not been generated. Scene timings are the baseline, not a requirement to rush a new narrator.

## Content boundaries

- Use “agentic,” “work,” and “workspace.” Don't replace the story with technical framework jargon.
- No PDF-editing promotion, complete Microsoft 365 replacement claim, or invented product functionality.
- Local storage does not establish local-only processing. Keep the cloud-processing qualifier.
- Current presentation/collaboration/agent scenes use prototype/design assets. Keep labels until genuine clips and feature confirmation are supplied.
- Real UI, explicit outcomes, selective review, collaboration, and agents are essential. The workspace remains the protagonist throughout.
