## Pain points → differentiator → marketing line
1. **AI made the document. Now I have to fix it.** Usable content, broken formatting (tables, slides). — "If AI saves you an hour writing, why does it take another hour to fix?"
2. **Why am I the one connecting everything?** Lawyer: email → old contracts → evidence → Word → annexure → email. Human is the orchestrator. — "You have AI for every tool. Why are you still doing all the work between them?"
3. **I can't trust what the AI changed.** What changed? Broken formulas? Removed clause? Source of figures? — "AI should show its work—not make you audit it from scratch."
4. **It worked on one file, not the whole project.** Excel assumptions + Word commentary + PPT deck treated as separate tasks. — "Your work is connected. Your AI should be too."
5. **AI tells me how. I need it to actually do it.** Advice/draft only; user still sources, edits, validates, assembles. — "Stop prompting for answers. Start delegating the work."
6. **Two-minute edit, AI takes forever.** Over-reasoning, regenerates sections, hallucinated changes. Diff: targeted structured edits, lightweight deterministic ops, proportionate verification. — "A small change shouldn't become a big project."
7. **AI sounds confident even when wrong.** Figures don't reconcile, invented citations. Diff: source-grounded workflows, independent verifier agents, deterministic validation, repair loops. — "Professional work needs more than confident answers. It needs verification."
8. **Do I give up control of my files?** Confidential docs. Positioning: local-first files, explicit access scopes, transparent cloud-model use *where supported by the implementation*. — "Your workspace. Your files. Your control."
9. **Why manage every AI assistant myself?** Research AI / drafting AI / slides AI; user transfers context. Diff: specialized agents via shared artifacts, managed memory, DAG orchestrator, verification. — "One request. A team of AI agents. One coordinated result."

**Top 5 by marketing potential:** 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.
