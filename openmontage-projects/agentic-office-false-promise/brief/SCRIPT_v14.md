# SCRIPT v14: "Introducing Diffs" (~2:00)

## What changed from v13, and why (user feedback, 2026-10-13)
- **"AI gave you the answer, you still have a board pack to fix" made no sense.** v14 SHOWS it instead:
  1. She asks the AI chat.
  2. It replies "Done ✓" with the new numbers, *inside the chat*.
  3. Her three files are untouched: "0 of 3 files updated".
  4. She carries every number across by hand.
- **The Meta stills didn't connect with the story.** Different woman, lamp and desk.
  - The spine is now the Canva Maya: navy blazer, white blouse, brass lamp, silver laptop, blue night windows.
  - The Meta stills are regenerated in her set.
  - The clock on every grind shot ties them into one night.
- **The pain wasn't intense enough.** The grind is longer, the clock climbs, the AI adds work ("Great question! Also update…"), the coffee goes cold and her eyes go, and she misses one box.
- **The ending wasn't happy.** She smiles closing the laptop, smiles at dawn, and David smiles at 9.
- **Graphics use the investor-deck theme:**
  - cream #F8F7F3, ink #161616, blue #2A63F8, rust #B8612A
  - Helvetica Neue, with letter-spaced small-caps labels
- **The match cut is the spine of the reversal.** The red ring David draws on slide 2 at 2:41 AM comes back as the ring on the very same row in the Diffs review.
