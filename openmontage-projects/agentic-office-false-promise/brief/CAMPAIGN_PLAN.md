# Agentic Office — "DONE!" campaign · plan v3

## Context
V27 (63s, `visuals/whatsapp_current_cut.mp4`) proves the product, but its pain act is flat UI mockups and nobody feels it. The goal is an **unforgettable** campaign in the class of OpenAI, Claude, Raycast and Linear. It centres on a 2–3 min hero film plus social cutdowns and a series, and the time it takes doesn't matter. Constraints:
- **$0 budget.** Gemini (2 videos/day, made by the user), free stock (Pexels/Mixkit), Remotion/HyperFrames, our Kling/Gemini clips, real Agentic Office UI.
- Film clips are allowed (the user accepts the risk), but only in the meme cut.
- Voice is local Kokoro.

Project root: `~/Desktop/OpenMontage/projects/agentic-office-false-promise/`.

## What the research says makes these ads unforgettable
- **Claude, "A Time and a Place"** (Mother; won the Cannes Film Grand Prix): **a real actor plays the AI**. A sincere request turns absurd, the UI never appears, and the end card is one contrast line.
- **Codex, "Time to Fly"**: it takes the users' own phrase ("feels like flying") **literally**. **"You Can Just Build Things"** was criticised because viewers couldn't tell what Codex does, so the product must be shown clearly in the middle.
- **ChatGPT, everyday spots** (35mm): small human goals, the prompt shown as type styled like film credits, and the end card shows the deliverable.
- **Harvey**: one physical symbol (the handshake) carries the brand.
- **Raycast and Linear**: the edit rhythm *is* the benefit (snappy means fast; slow holds mean control).
- **Google ABCD**: hook early, brand early, emotional connection, one clear CTA.

## The big idea
**Enemy:** AI that says "Done!" and hands you more work.
**Device:** the generic AI is **a person**, "DAN", an endlessly cheerful assistant whose only line is **"Done!"** Each "Done!" leaves things worse: a running gag built on the rule of three. Everyone has worked with a Dan.
**Hero:** Maya, a young associate, the night before a 9 AM hearing.
**Product truth:** Agentic Office doesn't say "done". It **shows you the change and lets you decide**.
**Signature brand asset:** the **tracked change with an Accept button** (red strike, green insert, a soft *click*). It recurs in every ad and is our handshake. The tagline itself is rendered as a diff:
~~AI said it was done.~~ → **This time, it is.** [Accept ✓]
That ends on: **Agentic Office — AI that works with your work. · Request early access.**
