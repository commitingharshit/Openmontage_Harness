The buzz, disbelief, paper-check and stillness beats are partly redundant; the missing face beat is a small moment of recognition when she sees “0 of 4 files changed,” before the bigger breakdown.

5. BUY: 6.5/10. The strongest proof is real UI showing the same model, memo and deck, tracked changes, and “10 of 10 accepted.” Missing is convincing proof that the deck preserves the real template and that changes propagate reliably across files—not just that a change list exists. The “AI drives” language, warm chord, “What if it worked the other way around?” title and happy CFO text are ad clichés. I would distrust the product because the core action is shown sped up, only acceptance is demonstrated, and “Accept all” makes the human review sound thinner than the promise of reviewing every change.

The “three files” line may briefly conflict with the visible four-file folder, even though the PDF is intentionally excluded.
