# 23 — Expansion Graph (wedge → vision)

*v1 · 2026-09-30. Each step lists the capability that unlocks it, the customers it adds, the revenue opportunity and the moat it strengthens. Revenue ranges are illustrative [A], tied to the market model where possible.*

```
[0] WEDGE: Recurring bespoke Office packs for fund / finance teams (+ the delivery teams that produce them)
 │   unlock: object link graph + deterministic roll-forward + tie-out + semantic review + restore
 ▼
[1] Adjacent packs in the same customers: portfolio monitoring, lender/covenant packs, IC memo updates, valuation reports
 │   unlock: PDF source ingestion with page anchors; pack templates; period model
 ▼
[2] Adjacent buyers with the same artifact: corporate FP&A board packs, credit funds, fund administrators, valuation advisors
 │   unlock: reviewer web surface; SSO/SOC 2; pack-based billing
 ▼
[3] Delivery-firm console: many client packs, QC queues, per-client model policy, overnight runs
 │   unlock: multi-tenant workspaces, SLAs, audit exports, white-label
 ▼
[4] Document-estate operations: apply a change across 100s of documents (policies, templates, contracts)
 │   unlock: batch jobs + the same diff/review machinery
 ▼
[5] Team & firm memory: cross-pack consistency, firm playbooks, handover of packs
 │   unlock: firm execution graph; correction learning across packs
 ▼
[6] Cross-application agents above M365/Google/data rooms: lineage and approval for AI-made changes anywhere
 │   unlock: connectors, policy engine, enterprise deployment
 ▼
[7] VISION: the operating layer for agentic knowledge work (matters, deals, engagements as the unit of work)
```

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 5 Firm memory | Cross-pack graph, firm playbooks | Existing customers | Enterprise upgrades ($120k+) | **Firm execution graph** (strongest) |
| 6 Cross-app control plane | Connectors, policy engine, lineage for any AI change | CIO/CFO governance buyers | Enterprise platform ($250k+) | Governance position above platforms |

**Sequencing rule**: do not start step N+1 until step N shows ≥100% NRR and second-cycle retention ≥80% in at least 10 customers. The only exception is step 3, which may start as a pilot in parallel with step 1 because it is also a GTM channel.
