# Executive summary

**The company.** Agentic Office is the execution-and-review layer for recurring multi-file professional work. When a period closes or a number changes, it keeps the Excel model, the decks, the memos and their source PDFs consistent. Every change is shown to a reviewer with its source, reason and impact, and the whole pack can be restored. The long-term vision is unchanged: the operating layer for agentic knowledge work.

**What changed this year, and why the thesis moved.** Between 22 April and 17 September 2026, Microsoft (Agent Mode GA), Anthropic (Claude for Word/Excel/PowerPoint GA, marketing "change one thing, and every open file catches up"), Google (Gemini cross-app agents) and OpenAI (ChatGPT in Word, including the free tier) all shipped agents that edit Office files. The original "Cursor for professional documents" story, and its signature demo, is now contested by the platform owners. Finance and legal verticals are held by very well-funded companies (Rogo ~$2B, Hebbia, Model ML $75M A, Harvey ~$16B, Legora $5.6B). We therefore narrowed the wedge.

**The wedge.** Recurring bespoke Office packs that must tie out every period: quarterly LP reports, portfolio-monitoring decks, IC updates, lender and covenant packs. These are built by fund-finance and portfolio-operations teams, and by the India-based production teams that serve them. The job is proven valuable: Workiva earned $885M in FY2025 (97.2% gross retention) keeping numbers consistent across documents, but it requires authoring inside Workiva at enterprise prices, and FP&A platforms (Datarails, Cube) generate packs only from their own data models. Session-scoped platform agents do not persist a cross-period object graph, a deterministic tie-out or pack-level restore.

**Which customer first, honestly.** Every candidate ICP is contested by a platform: fund-admin platforms (Juniper Square, Carta, Allvue) automate standardised LP statements, and FP&A platforms (Datarails, Cube) build corporate board packs from their data models. We therefore run a three-way interview race (fund-finance bespoke packs vs lender/credit reporting vs corporate FP&A board packs) and commit by day 45. The lead hypothesis is fund finance.

**Market.** Bottom-up TAM of **$4.9B** (BLS May-2025 occupations × document-intensity × $60/seat/month, plus other high-income markets and India). SAM of **$0.79B** (in-wedge finance and reporting roles with recurring packs). Two independent cross-checks give $10.8B TAM and up to $2.9B SAM. SOM is the operating plan's **$9.6M** year-5 ARR (base; conservative $2.5M, aggressive $33.4M). The two most uncertain inputs are document-intensity shares and the share of professionals with recurring packs; halving both cuts SAM to ~$0.20B.

**Business model.** Price the pack, not the seat. A typical agent-heavy user costs about $28/month in inference and a heavy user about $69 (base cost scenario), so $60 seats lose money on heavy users, while a $250/month pack earns ~74% gross margin *if* ~60% of updates run deterministically. The Rust kernels are a margin asset as well as a quality asset.

**Stage and evidence.** Prototype-stage, pre-revenue. Built: ≈50k lines of Rust OOXML kernels, ≈67k lines of agent runtime, a Code-OSS workbench, 17 office skills, a file-level dependency graph, 928 offline tests (July 2026). Not built: the wedge's core feature (object-level link graph, roll-forward, tie-out). Customer evidence: none yet.

**Recommendation.** (1) Commit to the pack wedge for 90 days. (2) Run 30 interviews, 5 pack teardowns and 2 side-by-side tests against Claude/Copilot, and sign ≥3 paid pilots. (3) Raise **$1.25M** pre-seed on a SAFE at an $8–12M post-money cap after ~6 weeks of validation, applying to YC / Accel Atoms / Surge in parallel. $1.25M funds the 18-month plan (net burn ≈$0.64M) and survives the conservative revenue case for 29 months. (4) Seed gates at ~month 18: ≥15 paying organisations, ≥$0.35M ARR, ≥80% retention across two cycles, ≥30% measured time saved.

**What would make us wrong.** Our ICP's packs live inside platforms rather than Office files; reviewers will not accept agent-made numeric changes; platform agents reach parity on a real roll-forward; or Microsoft/Anthropic ship persistent cross-file pack graphs first. Each is a day-90 kill or pivot criterion.

**Honest bottom line.** Today this is a strong engineering team with a capability lead and no moat. It is fundable at pre-seed only with a sharp wedge and early customer evidence, and venture-scale only if the pack graph and learned playbooks compound faster than the platforms can generalise them.
