# Financial model

`models/15_FINANCIAL_MODEL.xlsx`: monthly for 60 months (Jan 2027 – Dec 2031), three scenarios, every driver editable (Inputs IDs F/R/C/O), headcount by role and hire month, plus Annual, Snapshot and FundingStress sheets. All revenue is forecast from hypotheses; there are no customers today.

## Revenue logic
Paid pilots (base: 4 at $2.5k/month, months 4–9, 60% converting) → founder-led mid-market logos (1/month from month 7) → ramped AEs (first AE month 10; 1.5 deals per ramped AE per month at $15k ACV) → enterprise AEs after the seed ($120k ACV) → a small self-serve Pro line. Monthly churn 1.5% and expansion 1.8% (mid-market).
