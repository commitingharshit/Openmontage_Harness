# BULL CASE — what must be true (bottom-up)

*v1 · 2026-09-30. Not a forecast. Each rung lists the arithmetic and the conditions that must hold. Base-case model reaches $9.6M exit ARR in year 5; aggressive $33.4M (15_FINANCIAL_MODEL).*

## Rung 1 — $10M ARR (≈ year 4–5 base; month 41 aggressive)
**Arithmetic (one plausible mix):**
- 350 mid-market fund/finance teams × $20k ACV (≈4–6 packs + platform fee) = **$7.0M**
- 25 enterprise / delivery-firm accounts × $120k = **$3.0M**
- ≈375 organisations. The US mid-market alone has ~200k companies (NCMM), and ~12k US investment managers are assumed (A021), so this is **<1%** penetration of eligible organisations.

**Must be true:**
1. H1 and H6: the pack job exists in Office files and reviewers trust agent-made numeric changes.
2. Pilot→paid ≥60%; second-cycle retention ≥80%; NRR ≥110% (via more packs per customer).
3. 6–8 productive AEs at ≥1.5 deals/month, plus 1–2 delivery-firm partnerships.
4. Gross margin ≥75% with ≥60% of numeric updates deterministic.
5. Platforms do not ship persistent cross-period pack graphs before ~year 3.

## Rung 2 — $100M ARR (≈ year 7–9 if things go right)
**Arithmetic (one plausible mix):**
- 3,000 mid-market organisations × $20k = **$60M** (≈3% of ~100k eligible US mid-market + funds, before non-US)
- 250 enterprise accounts × $160k (finance + reporting + strategy teams; private deployment) = **$40M**
- Or equivalently ~4.5M M365 seats' worth of pack work at ~$2/seat/month, i.e. the long tail of reporting teams Workiva never reached (Workiva itself: $885M on 6,624 customers).

**Must be true:**
1. **Expansion beyond the wedge works**: lender/covenant packs, corporate board packs, valuation reports, audit support and document-estate jobs all run on the same engine (23_EXPANSION_GRAPH steps 1–4).
2. **The playbook moat forms**: correction rates fall cycle over cycle, and packs per customer rise to 8+.
3. **Channel leverage**: delivery firms and fund administrators run many client packs on Agentic Office (the white-label/console model), which is the cheapest distribution.
4. Enterprise readiness: SOC 2 Type II, private cloud, BYO model, connectors (SharePoint, Box, data rooms).
5. Coexistence with Microsoft: Marketplace listing and co-sell rather than head-on competition.

## Rung 3 — a $1B+ company
- **Valuation path**: at 2026 multiples (Harvey ~40–50× ARR, Gamma ~21×, Workiva public ~5–7× revenue), a $1B valuation needs roughly **$60–100M ARR growing ≥40%** with ≥120% NRR, or ~$30–50M ARR with exceptional growth in a hot market.
- **Vision path (ARR ≥ $1B, the "operating layer")**: ~50,000 organisations × $20k, or 1% of M365 commercial seats (4.5M) × ~$20/month. That requires steps 5–7 of the expansion graph: firm execution memory, a cross-application control plane governing AI-made changes to documents everywhere, and matters/deals/engagements as the unit of work.

**Must be true for the vision:**
1. AI agents produce most first drafts and updates of professional documents by 2030, so **governance, lineage and review of AI-made changes** becomes a budget line (much as code review and CI/CD did for software).
2. The platform owners stay per-app or per-vendor, and customers want a **neutral** layer across Microsoft, Google, Anthropic, OpenAI and open-weight models.
3. Agentic Office owns the **review and lineage data** for enough packs that its playbooks and firm graphs are materially better than generic memory.

## Why the bull case is not absurd
- Workiva built an ~$885M/year business on "numbers must tie across documents" *before* agents. Agents multiply the number of document changes that need tying out and reviewing.
- DataSnipper reached ~600k users and a $1B valuation on evidence-to-cell linking inside Excel alone.
- Cursor showed that an interaction layer over existing files (a Git repository) can be worth more than the editor underneath it.

## What would make the bull case collapse
Any one of: Microsoft or Anthropic ship persistent cross-file pack graphs with review/restore before we have ~50 customers; H1/H6 fail; NRR stays <100% (packs per customer do not grow).
