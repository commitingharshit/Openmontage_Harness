# What we want: the Agentic Office launch film (v4 brief)

*Written 2026-10-12, after v3 was rejected: "I can't understand where AI is causing the problem. The workflow is not shown."*

## 1. Why v1–v3 didn't work
- They showed **a stressed person**, not **the workflow** that stresses her.
- "AI fails" was **abstract**: red marks, a cheerful man in orange. Nobody could say *what the AI did wrong*.
- Messages flashed by too fast to read, so the story lived in text nobody saw.
- The real pain from our deck never appeared: **AI answers in a chat, while the work sits in folders, so the human carries everything across.**

## 2. The one insight the film must land
> **Today, you drive and the AI suggests.**
> You upload the files, explain the context, copy the answer, paste it into Word, fix the formatting, update the model by hand, rebuild the deck the chatbot "made", and check everything yourself.
>
> **With Agentic Office it's reversed: the AI drives and you approve.**
> It works *inside* your folder (model, memo and deck together), shows every change, and you accept or reject.

Everything in the film either shows the human doing the AI's job, or shows the AI doing the job while the human approves.

## 4. Audience
Fund-finance and portfolio-ops associates, FP&A, bankers and consultants who build recurring board or LP packs. Secondary: AI power users. Feed: LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Instagram.

## 5. Rules
1. **Show the workflow on screen**, at the pace a real person would read it. Every message holds for at least 2.5 s and is large enough to read on a phone.
2. **One task, one number, start to finish:** "DACH Q4 to 8.5 → FY26 €74.6m (was €71–73m)."
3. **The AI's failure must be specific and true to life:** an answer only in chat, files unchanged; a downloaded deck in the wrong template; pasted text with stray `**` markdown; three `_updated(2).pptx` files.
4. **No real AI brand names or logos** ("AI chat", not ChatGPT or Claude). Narration may say "today's chatbots".
5. **Product proof only from real app footage.** Prototype mockups are labelled as such or not used. Never show the internal name "Hermes", file paths, or the failed-step span.
6. **Faces only where they add emotion**: Maya's reactions, not plot. If Dan the AI is used, every Dan beat must be paired with a screen showing exactly what the AI did (Version B).
7. One ending, one line, one CTA.

## 6. Versions we want
| Version | Length | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **A: "Role Reversal"** (main) | ~75 s, 16:9 | Screen-led truth plus Maya's reactions; narrator |
| **B: "Dan"** | ~75 s | The same story, with the AI played by Dan for comedy; each Dan beat cuts to the screen showing what he actually did |
| **C: Sound-off** | ~75 s | Version A with captions in place of narration (for feeds that autoplay muted) |
| **D: 9:16 short** | ~30 s | The reversal only: "You drive, AI suggests" → "AI drives, you approve" |

## 7. Success test
Show it, with no explanation, to five people from the audience. They should be able to say:
1. "She had to update one number across the model, memo and deck."
2. "The chatbot only told her what to do, so she did it all by hand, and its deck was broken."
3. "Agentic Office did it inside her files and she just approved the changes."
4. They remember the name.
