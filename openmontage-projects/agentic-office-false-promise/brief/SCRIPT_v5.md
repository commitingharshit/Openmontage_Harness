# SCRIPT v5: "You are the operating system"

*Draft for the critic, then for approval. Nothing gets built until this is approved. v5 does what the v4 notes asked: **more acts** (6), a much **louder Act 1 pain** that keeps building, and **Maya's face carries each step** (hope → doubt → disbelief → exhaustion → stillness → resolve → relief). The real complaints behind each beat are in `RESEARCH_PAIN.md` (cited as [R#]).*

**The idea in one line.** Today you drive and the AI suggests: it answers in a chat, while your work sits in files it never touches. Agentic Office reverses that: the AI drives inside your files, and you approve.

**The one task (the same number from first frame to last).** David: *"DACH Q4 is now 8.5. Model, memo and deck need the new FY26 number. Board at 9."* Old: **€71–73m**. New: **€74.6m**.

**Main cut ~2:00 (timings below run to 2:04; trim beat 26 to 2 s and beat 28 to 4 s to land on 2:00) · Director's cut ~2:30 (adds the ⊕ beats) · 75 s cut and 30 s 9:16 at the end.**

## Devices that run through the film
- **The clock** (top-right super, white on dark): 11:47 PM → 3:12 AM → 6:04 AM. Every jump is a hard cut.
- **The Operating System panel** (left rail, Acts 2–4). These are the 7 steps from the deck's slide 2. Each lights up as she does it, and an action counter ticks underneath:
  `1 Read the request · 2 Find the emails · 3 Prompt the AI · 4 Move the draft into Word · 5 Update related files · 6 Propagate to the deck · 7 Cross-check by hand`
  Footer, in two columns: **YOU: n actions** | **AI: 0 edits in your files**. The AI number never moves in Acts 2–4. That frozen zero is the joke and the wound.
- **The three taxes** appear once each as a stamp, in the deck's own words: **CONTEXT TAX** (uploads), **EXECUTION GAP** (0 files changed), **REVIEW TAX** (cross-check).
- **Readability rule**: every chat message, email and text holds for **at least 2.5 s**, is set at least 42 px high at 1080p, and is typed or streamed at a speed a person can read.
