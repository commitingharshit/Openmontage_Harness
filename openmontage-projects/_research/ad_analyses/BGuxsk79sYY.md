## Connections
- **One continuous 3D card-space**: from 0:00 to 0:43 there are only 3 hard cuts. Every "transition" is the camera moving through the same layer of floating cards.
- **Typed prompts** act as dialogue: the cursor types, and the image answers.
- **Garden → barn → animated barn** is one asset evolving, so cause and effect are visible.
- Short declarative supers (two words, period) form a rhythmic voice: "Add characters. Build environments. Create stories."
- The montage cuts land on the music drop, and the title stacks after it.

## Top 5 transferable techniques
1. A continuous 3D "card space" instead of cuts.
2. Type the prompt on screen and let the result answer it.
3. Three parallel two-word supers (verb + noun).
4. A slow 40 s build, then an 8 s rapid montage on the drop.
5. Delay the product name until the montage climax.
