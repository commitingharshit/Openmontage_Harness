## Connections
- **Grid morph**: the floating sizzle thumbnails land as the asset grid, a match-transition from promise to tool.
- **One narrative project** ("Cloud Sailor": ship → steel ship → whale → helmsman → parrot → mast) runs through every feature, so the tutorial reads as a short film.
- **Blur pulse** (defocus → refocus) stands in for "generating…" and works as a soft cut.
- The end strip recaps the whole story.

## Hook
The wordmark is lit by moving, saturated gens, and the background changes every 1.3 s.

## Product
UI from 0:37. Lasso and Insert edits are shown as in-place before/after.

## Pacing
43 cuts, 44 shots, about 4.3 s average. 33 cuts happen in the first 37 s (~1.1 s, the fastest burst ~0.3 s at 0:16–0:19), then about 8 cuts in 2:20 of animated screen capture.

## Visual language
Pure-black canvas, floating rounded cards, deep defocus, Google Sans, soft white glow. Gens are natural-light photoreal plus playful stylized pieces.

## Sound
Upbeat bed ducked under the VO (a flat −16 LUFS throughout), swelling at the open and close.

## Top 5 transferable techniques
1. Morph the sizzle montage into the product UI.
2. One mini-story carried through every feature.
3. A blur-pulse as the "it's working" transition.
4. A sizzle cut that accelerates to sub-0.5 s before the first VO beat.
5. Recap as a scrolling film strip before the logo.
