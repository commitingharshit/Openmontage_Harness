## Connections
- Colour is the story: B&W = conformity, teal colour = freedom; the grade change is the transition.
- Graphic matches: crowd flow ↔ powder spray.
- The epilogue fuses the two worlds in one frame.

## Hook
One word spoken and typed over black names the enemy before any image; the cryptic word makes you wait.

## Product / CTA
One goggle macro (58 s); brand only after the tagline. Sells identity, not features.

## Pacing
75 cuts / 96.9 s → **ASL 1.27 s**; uneven: ~25 cuts in 32–37 s, ~6 in 60–92 s. Audio stays −10 to −13 dB until ~68 s, then falls to −44 dB: silence is the release.

## Visual language
Monochrome vs cyan-teal grade, letterbox, telephoto crowds, low wide angles on the skier, sun flares, slow-mo.

## Sound
Whispered VO, pulsing score time-stretched on "sound stretches", whoosh hits on cuts, then near silence.

## Top 5 transferable techniques
1. Two-world intercut, encoded by grade (B&W = problem, colour = product world).
2. Spaced-serif subtitle VO as the main typography.
3. A stutter-cut crescendo, then one long held wide shot.
4. Drop the music to silence for the emotional release.
5. A post-card epilogue that blends the two worlds.
