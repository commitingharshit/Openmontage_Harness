## Connections
- The circle motif: lens → thumbnail wall → recap wall. Every section exits through a circle or blur.
- Prompt-to-world loop repeated five times with rising novelty.
- Recap triad echoes the section supers.

## Hook

## Top 5 transferable techniques
1. Prompt → result loop, repeated with escalating novelty.
2. A single shape motif (circle) as both transition and title wall.
3. Silent text-only cold open with "And now…" before the music drop.
4. One-line supers as chapter heads, recapped as a triad at the end.
5. Live-action hands/phone inserts to ground software demos.
