## Connections
- Rule-of-three title escalation. The card colour (teal → red → white) codes each chapter.
- Benchmark callback: every ship passes the same Saturn shot and the same trajectory grid, so the gains are readable.
- The VO's last word lands on the card ("learn how to go… **fast**").
- Dissolves and fades through black rather than hard cuts.

## Hook
A quiet black screen with a quote: restraint and authority. That suits long form, but it is too slow for social.

## Product
The ring station is foreshadowed at 0:26. The hero arrives at 2:54 (62% in), after two inferior versions. The end card is logo + URL, with no spoken CTA.

## Pacing
11 hard cuts at 0.3 (18 at 0.15), so the average shot is about 15–23 s. The edit doesn't speed up; the score and camera speed do, in the warp block. It slows again for the arrival.

## Visual language
Photoreal CG, deep blacks, one hard sun key with rim-lit hulls, a desaturated teal-and-sand grade. The wide frame keeps ships tiny against planets. Wide-tracked thin caps for titles, small HUD labels.

## Sound
Orchestral/synth score climbing from −32 to −13 LUFS, then falling. Calm, sparse documentary VO with music gaps before each card.

## Top 5 transferable techniques
1. Escalating chapter cards (good → better → ours), each colour-coded.
2. A constant benchmark shot every option is tested against.
3. Show the alternatives' limits before the reveal.
4. End the VO line on the card's word.
5. Small-against-vast scale, then let the hero fill the frame.
