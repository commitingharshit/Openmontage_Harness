## Connections
- **Payoff after each feature**: every UI step resolves in a full-screen generated clip, so the cuts alternate tool → result.
- **One continuous character** (lava-lamp woman) runs from feature 2 to the finale and proves consistency.
- **Bokeh continuity**: the UI always floats over a blurred version of the current gen, so the space feels continuous and there are no hard UI cuts.
- VO "So let's jump right in" is the verbal bridge out of the sizzle.

## Hook
The wordmark sits over three surreal macro gens in 4 s: brand and capability in one image.

## Product
The product is the UI. It appears at 0:23 after the sizzle.

## Pacing
30 cuts, 31 shots, about 5.6 s average, but split in two: 17 cuts in the first 23 s (~1.35 s) and only about 9 in the remaining 2:30 (long screen-recording takes animated with zooms).

## Visual language
Dark UI on blurred colour fields, rounded cards, soft shadows, Google Sans, white glowing wordmark. Gens are high-saturation macro and surreal.

## Sound
Light electronic bed, ducked under a calm, friendly VO. It swells at the open and close. No UI SFX.

## Top 5 transferable techniques
1. A sizzle of results before any UI.
2. Tool step → full-screen result, every time.
3. A blurred-backdrop "UI in space" keeps demos cinematic.
4. One hero character threaded through the features.
5. Credentials as builder supers (A + B + C).
